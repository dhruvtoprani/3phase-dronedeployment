3-Phase Drone Deployment is a robotics systems prototype for staged deployment, coordination logic, and operational planning.

It explores how a robotics workflow can be broken into phases, constraints, and decision points before scaling the implementation. The project reflects a PM-style approach to technical systems: understand the operating environment, map the workflow, and define what the system needs to support.

## Product Signal

Robotics products are rarely just about the robot. They depend on planning, constraints, sequencing, and operational handoffs. This prototype shows curiosity around the full system layer that makes robotics usable in real environments.

## What I Explored

- Staged deployment logic for robotics workflows
- Coordination tradeoffs across phases of operation
- How operational constraints shape system design
- Turning a robotics idea into a concrete technical sketch
